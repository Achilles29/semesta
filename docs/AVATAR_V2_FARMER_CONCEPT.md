# SEMESTA FARMER V2 — male base concept brief

## Character

An original young-adult Indonesian male farmer for SEMESTA. Warm brown skin, layered chestnut hair, large expressive dark eyes, a sculpted friendly face, a medium-blue work shirt with readable collar/cuffs/seams, olive-green trousers, and practical brown boots. Approx. 3.5–4 heads tall. Neutral A-pose, palms relaxed and open for future tool-grip poses. No hat, backpack, facial hair, tools, or handheld props in the base modeling pass.

## Reference sheet request

Use the art direction in `AVATAR_V2_STYLE_GUIDE.md`. Generate one orthographic-style model sheet with the same design in separate aligned panels: front, left profile, right profile, back, front three-quarter, and face close-up. Match camera scale, lighting, body proportions, hairstyle, face, fabric details, and colors across panels. Full body including planted boot soles in each full-body panel. Neutral studio background and soft neutral lighting. Original design only; no named franchise/game references, logos, text, prop, dramatic foreshortening, or alternate outfit. Do not present the generated sheet as a model or game asset.

## Intended source handoff

After human review of the concept, create a genuinely modeled/retopologized character, UV unwrap it, texture it, rig it to `semesta-humanoid-v2.0`, weight-paint it, animate Idle/Walk/Run/Interact, and export a GLB. The concept sheet is only a modeling guide. A usable 3D source must be delivered separately as licensed `.blend`, `.fbx`, `.obj` plus material textures, or `.glb`.
